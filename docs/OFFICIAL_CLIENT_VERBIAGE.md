# Official Client Verbiage — Live Achieve Believe (LAB)

*This is the exact, approved verbiage provided by the client for the entire website.*

---

## 1. HOW WE SERVE (Core Manifesto)

> **Love is more than something we believe. It is something we choose to do. Every day.**

Every act of service begins with the same belief: every person deserves to be seen, valued, and given the opportunity to thrive.

Whether we're sharing a meal, helping someone turn a house into a home, investing in young people, creating conversations through the arts, or standing beside a veteran, our purpose never changes.

Only the way we serve does.

---

## 2. A Place at the Table (Door 1)

**Lead:** Sometimes hope begins with a meal.

A meal is never just food. It is an invitation to belong, to be heard, and to be reminded that every person has value.

### What We Do
LAB provides meals and compassionate outreach to individuals experiencing homelessness, veterans, and families facing difficult circumstances. Through community partnerships and direct street outreach, we share nourishment, encouragement, and moments of genuine human connection.

Our efforts include serving alongside Bread of Refuge and Family Promise of Hillsborough County, as well as bringing meals directly to people in need.

### How You Can Help
Help prepare or serve meals, donate food and supplies, sponsor a meal outreach, or connect us with community partners who share our commitment to serving others.

**Tagline:** There Is Always Another Seat Waiting.  
**CTAs:** Volunteer | Donate Food & Supplies | Sponsor a Meal

---

## 3. Strong Foundations (Door 2 / You Matter / Youth)

**Lead:** Sometimes hope begins with guidance.

Every young person deserves to know their worth, discover their strengths, and recognize the possibilities ahead.

### What We Do
Through our LEADS Youth Development partnership with Life Skills, Empowerment and Development Services, LAB helps young people build the skills that support healthy relationships and stronger futures.

The program emphasizes communication, respect, confidence, resilience, and practical life skills that help youth make thoughtful choices and navigate life’s challenges.

### How You Can Help
Support youth development activities, share relevant professional skills, provide educational resources and program supplies, or help sponsor opportunities that encourage young people to learn and grow.

**Tagline:** Because believing in a young person today can change how they see tomorrow.  
**CTAs:** Volunteer | Support Youth Development | Become a Program Sponsor

---

## 4. Stories That Save Lives (Door 3 / Suicide Awareness Showdown)

**Lead:** Sometimes hope begins with one conversation.

Behind every smile is a story we may not know. Sometimes the most important words are the ones someone hasn't yet found the courage to say.

### What We Do
Through Suicide Awareness Showdown and other creative experiences, LAB uses storytelling, theatre, music, and dance to encourage meaningful conversations about mental health, emotional struggles, human connection, and hope.

Our goal is to help people recognize what others may be carrying, reduce the silence surrounding difficult experiences, and encourage people to seek support before reaching a crisis.

We also welcome collaboration with mental health professionals and community organizations to help connect audiences with appropriate educational resources and support.

### How You Can Help
Volunteer your time or talents, participate in productions and events, help with event operations, become a sponsor, contribute professional expertise, or help us connect with mental health organizations and community partners.

**Tagline:** Every Story Matters. Every Life Has Value.  
**CTAs:** Volunteer | Sponsor an Event | Partner With Us

---

## 5. Welcome Home (Door 4 / Veteran Housing)

**Lead:** Sometimes hope begins with a key.

Home is more than a roof overhead. It is a place to feel safe, regain stability, and begin imagining what comes next.

### What We Do
LAB works to create welcoming, furnished housing opportunities for veterans who need stability and a comfortable place to call home.

We seek suitable rental properties, furnish homes with essential household items, and establish property-care arrangements that support comfortable living while respecting the responsibilities of property owners.

Our vision is to provide more than a place to stay. We want veterans to experience dignity, peace of mind, and a foundation from which to move forward.

### How You Can Help
Property owners and real estate professionals can help us identify suitable homes. Businesses and individuals can donate furnishings, household necessities, or financial support. Community partners can also help connect veterans with appropriate services and resources.

**Tagline:** Because those who have served deserve a place where they feel valued.  
**CTAs:** Explore a Housing Partnership | Donate Furnishings | Support Veteran Housing

## 6. A New Beginning (Door 5 / Essentials)

**Lead:** Sometimes hope begins with the little things.

A fresh set of linens. A pair of shoes. A table where a family can gather. What seems ordinary to one person can mean a new beginning to another.

### What We Do
LAB helps individuals and families access practical necessities, including clothing, shoes, household goods, linens, and other everyday essentials.

These items can ease financial burdens, help turn an empty space into a welcoming home, and give someone the confidence and comfort to take their next step.

### How You Can Help
Donate usable clothing, shoes, furniture, linens, and household essentials; organize a collection drive; help sort and prepare donated items; or contribute toward purchasing supplies for specific needs.

**Tagline:** Sometimes a new beginning starts with something beautifully simple.  
**CTAs:** Donate Essentials | Organize a Drive | Volunteer

## 7. Serve Beside Us (Volunteers & Community)

**Lead:** Sometimes hope begins with someone who says yes.

You don't need extraordinary resources to make an extraordinary difference. Sometimes all it takes is a willing heart and the decision to show up.

### What We Do
LAB brings volunteers together to support community meals, outreach activities, youth initiatives, housing preparation, donation organization, special events, and other opportunities to serve.

We seek to match people’s talents, interests, and availability with meaningful ways to contribute.

### How You Can Help
Join us as an individual volunteer, bring your family or a group, organize a workplace service activity, or share a professional skill that can strengthen our work.

Whether you have a few hours or an ongoing commitment to offer, we welcome the opportunity to explore how you can serve alongside us.

**Tagline:** We don't believe everyone has to do everything. We believe everyone can do something.  
**CTAs:** Become a Volunteer | Bring a Group | Share Your Talents

## 8. Your Story (Partners & Donors)

**Lead:** Every story has the power to change another.

You've walked through our story. Now there is room for yours.

### What We Do
LAB brings individuals, businesses, faith communities, organizations, and community leaders together around a shared belief that people matter.

Through partnerships, sponsorships, charitable giving, and community involvement, we work to expand the reach of our programs and create more opportunities for people to experience compassion in action.

Every contribution matters, whether it is time, talent, resources, relationships, or encouragement.
