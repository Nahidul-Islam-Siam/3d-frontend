# LIVE ACHIEVE BELIEVE — GLOBAL DESIGN SYSTEM RULES
   
## 1. Global Typography
* **Titles & Headings (`h1`, `h2`, `h3`, `h4`, `h5`, `h6`)**: `Playfair Display`, serif.
* **Buttons (`button`, `[role="button"]`, `.btn`)**: `Playfair Display`, serif.
* **Paragraphs, Body, Lists, Links, Form Inputs, Badges, & All Others**: `Helvetica`, `Arial`, sans-serif.

## 2. Core Palette
* **Primary Button Color**: `#F97316` (Hover: `#ea580c`)
* **Primary Brand Accent**: Orange (`#ea580c` / `#f97316`)
* **Warm Canvas**: Cream (`#fbf8f2` / `#faf6ee`)
* **Border Line**: Soft warm stroke (`border-orange-200/90` or `border-orange-100`)
* **Text Tone**: Deep charcoal (`#1c1917` / `#292524`)
* **Dark Surfaces**: Obsidian black (`#050505` / `#09090b`)
